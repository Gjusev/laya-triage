Multilingual support ticket triage on a local laya model. Every answer carries a calibrated confidence, and any ticket the model is unsure about goes to a human, with the reason attached.

01 — the pipeline

Coarse to fine, signals riding along

A flat choice over 77 intents collapses (36.5% measured). So the model first picks one of 12 areas, then the exact intent inside that area. Urgency, frustration, churn and refund signals are answered in the same pass as the coarse choice.

03 — the escalation policy

Auto-handle what you can prove. Escalate the rest.

The threshold is chosen from a measured coverage/accuracy curve, not a guess: at 0.84, the model keeps 60.5% of tickets and answers 75.2% of those correctly. Every escalated ticket carries its reason.
